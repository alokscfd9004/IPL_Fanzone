2. Your rights (GDPR)

  • Access & portability (Art. 15/20): download everything we hold as JSON.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): one click on the My Data page wipes server records + cookies.
  • Restriction (Art. 18): choose “Decline” in the cookie banner — we stop persisting personalisation data.
  • Lodging a complaint: you may complain to your local supervisory authority (e.g. ICO in the UK, your EU DPA).

Lawful bases: consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for optional personalisation & Jarvis memory; legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) for security/rate-limit counters.

3. Your rights (CCPA / CPRA — California)

  • Right to know / delete / correct: use the My Data page — it does all three.
  • Right to opt-out of sale/sharing: we do not sell or share personal information. There is nothing to opt out of — but declining cookies also disables the third-party embeds below.
  • Non-discrimination: the site works fully even if you decline cookies (Jarvis just won't remember you between visits).

5. Cookies we set

  • sessionid — strictly necessary (rate limiting, CSRF session).
  • csrftoken — strictly necessary (form security).
  • fz_consent — your accept/decline choice (180 days).

Fan name/team use localStorage, not cookies, and only after you consent.

6. Data security & transfers

User identifiers are SHA-256 hashed before use in rate-limit counters. AI prompts go to Groq (US) — no names are required, so prompts are effectively anonymous. Backups: none beyond the host's standard disk.
